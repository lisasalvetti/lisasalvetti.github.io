Research

Ongoing projects:

Keep It Clean! Civic Engagement and Norm Enforcement in the City
(with Fabio Galeotti, Astrid Hopfensitz, and Cesar Mantilla)

This project examines whether participating in a public clean-up changes people's willingness to enforce social norms in public spaces.

The Effect of Climate Fresk on Environmental Knowledge, Intentions, and Behaviors: Field Evidence from an International Company
(with Simon Briole, Marion Davin, Dimitri Dubois, Lisette Ibanez, Sébastien Roussel, and Lisa Salvetti)

This project evaluates how Climate Fresk workshops affect employees' environmental knowledge, intentions, and behaviors in an international company.

Does Awareness Enter the Wardrobe?
(with Lisette Ibanez and Sébastien Roussel)

This project explores whether greater awareness of the environmental impact of clothing translates into changes in clothing-related choices.
